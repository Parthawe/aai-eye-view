For Mom

Mom, when you told me that the right half of what you see through your left eye goes black, I wanted to understand what that feels like. You said faces are hard to make out until people come closer. I made this so we could look at it together and talk about what feels familiar.

What is the viewer showing?

The left-eye view is blurry at the left edge and gradually gets darker toward the right corner. In camera mode, frame a face on the visible side and try moving the camera a little closer to see what details you can make out. National Eye Institute: Low vision

What do glasses change?

Glasses can correct blur caused by the eye not focusing light properly. The toggle illustrates a small focus adjustment layered over the two views. National Eye Institute: Refractive errors

How do we find out if vision has changed?

Ask the eye clinic what diagnosis they recorded, which eye is affected, what the injections are treating, and whether vision or exam findings have changed across visits. Ask what to do if redness and worse vision occur together, particularly after an injection. The viewer can’t answer those questions.

Camera and privacy

Camera access starts only when you press the camera button and grant permission. The site processes frames in this tab, doesn’t record or upload them, and never requests the microphone. Use “Stop camera” to go back to the illustrated scene.
