02 / The story

For Mom

I made this because I wanted to understand what you meant when you described a dark spot in the middle of what you see. It’s a way for us to look at something together and talk about what feels familiar.

What is the viewer showing?

A blind spot in the middle of the visual field is called a central scotoma. A change in central vision can make it harder to read a word or make out a face you’re looking straight at. The patch is fixed at the center of each image to illustrate that idea; this page doesn’t track where you look. National Eye Institute: Low vision

What do glasses change?

Glasses can correct blur caused by the eye not focusing light properly. Here, switching glasses on removes an added focus blur from both views. The illustrated central patch stays in place. The toggle doesn’t model a prescription. National Eye Institute: Refractive errors

How do we find out if vision has changed?

Ask the eye clinic what diagnosis they recorded, which eye is affected, what the injections are treating, and whether vision or exam findings have changed across visits. Ask what to do if redness and worse vision occur together, particularly after an injection. The viewer can’t answer those questions.
