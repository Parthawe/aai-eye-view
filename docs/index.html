What is the viewer showing?

The left view fades from blur to darkness. Try the camera, then choose Subtle or Strong. National Eye Institute: Low vision

What do glasses change?

The glasses option makes a small focus adjustment. National Eye Institute: Refractive errors

How do we find out if vision has changed?

Her eye clinic can compare exams and explain changes in her vision.

Help

  1. Start camera

    Tap Start camera and allow camera access. Both squares should show live video. Buttons hide unless Keep controls visible is on.

  2. Turn your phone sideways

    The two eye views should stay square and side by side. Some browsers keep the address bar visible.

  3. Tap either eye view

    Controls should appear. After five idle seconds they hide again. Try the Glasses switch. To read at your own pace, open Adjust, choose More, and turn on Keep controls visible.

  4. Adjust

    Choose Blur, Dark area, or More. Use the sliders or + and − buttons to change the view. The values and image should change. The panel stays open while you use it.

  5. Done

    Tap Done to close Adjust. The camera should keep running. The right eye starts at 2/5 blur on a fresh visit. Your saved adjustments take priority.

  6. Close view

    Tap an eye view, then Close view. You should return to Start camera, and your phone’s camera indicator should turn off.

Ask Mom what feels familiar and where it should look blurrier or darker.

If something fails, note your phone model, browser, and which step caused the problem.
